# Japanese content source attribution

The generated data reports and samples contain material from:

- [OpenJLPT](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT), CC BY-SA 4.0: candidate corpus, meanings, example associations, community levels, supplementary classifications, and vocabulary IDs. Its [NOTICE](https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT/blob/0d1d3410bec90bd4098a7c72de820543cb4f707c/NOTICE.md) describes field-level provenance.
- [Jonathan Waller's JLPT Resources](https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), CC BY: community vocabulary/kanji classifications and short vocabulary glosses. [Sharing statement](https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/sharing/).
- [stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab), CC BY-SA 4.0: reference CSVs, dictionary IDs, and corrections derived from Waller's lists. [Attribution and limitations](https://github.com/stephenmk/yomitan-jlpt-vocab/blob/b062d4e38c4bdd0950ae1d4ec55f04b176182e03/README.md).
- [JMdict and KANJIDIC2, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group](https://www.edrdg.org/), CC BY-SA 4.0 under the [EDRDG dictionary licence](https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html): dictionary spellings/readings and character readings/meanings. Jim Breen and the EDRDG contributors retain their upstream credits.
- [scriptin/jmdict-simplified](https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified): JSON conversion of the full JMdict English and KANJIDIC2 English dictionaries. The dictionary data retain their upstream licence; see the project's [licence documentation](https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified#license).
- [Tatoeba contributors](https://tatoeba.org/), CC BY 2.0 FR: example sentences supplied through OpenJLPT. Each `tatoeba_id` links to the Japanese sentence and contributors at `https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>`. [Corpus reuse guidance](https://en.wiki.tatoeba.org/articles/show/using-the-tatoeba-corpus).

Derived corpus data in `reports/`, `vocabulary-corrections.json`, `reference-decisions.json` and the compiled draft `flashcards-v4.json` are shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with upstream credits retained. Changes include audit annotations, review states, stable identities, dictionary-compatible pairs, explicit construction/phrase evidence, reference-coverage decisions and the recorded spelling/link/gloss/POS/example corrections. The 113 reference additions derive their selected meanings/POS/usage notes from JMdict, their community level and row provenance from the reference CSVs, and retain the original reference definitions. Fourteen character-reading links cite existing KANJIDIC2-verified source cards. These additions do not claim an OpenJLPT origin or official JLPT status. This does not grant rights to the citation-only source works described below. Downloaded source files are not modified. Corrected cards preserve complete original content and sentence IDs; unrelated examples are removed only from effective content, without generated replacements. These are development research/import artifacts, not endorsed or official JLPT material. No audio is included.

Complete-expression attestations cite these additional sources, retrieved 2026-10-23:

- [The Japan Foundation, IRODORI Elementary 1 vocabulary list (English)](https://www.irodori-online.jpf.go.jp/a2-1/references/vocabulary/assets/pdf/reference-vocabulary-list-en.pdf), PDF pages 15 and 31: 少々お待ちください and お元気で, with readings and meaning evidence. Scanned pages were visually checked.
- [日本語検定委員会, 敬語 explanation, 2015-04-24](https://www.nihongokentei.jp/check/level4/keigo-4-1.html): ご存じです（か） as a respectful question. No test question or options are imported, and this provider's 4級 is not treated as JLPT N4.
- [小学館, デジタル大辞泉, 行ってらっしゃい, via コトバンク](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E8%A1%8C%E3%81%A4%E3%81%A6%E3%82%89%E3%81%A4%E3%81%97%E3%82%84%E3%81%84-680807): the expanded common expression 行っていらっしゃい and departure-greeting evidence.

These three works retain their publishers' rights. They are cited for short common expressions and authored lexical notes; no permission to redistribute or relicense the full works is asserted. Full PDF/HTML bytes stay in the ignored cache. The ledger records SHA-256 hashes, source URLs, publishers, locators, retrieval date, citation-only use and the two explicit quotation normalizations. Do not interpret the corpus's CC BY-SA notice as a licence for those source pages or illustrations.

Exact corpus revisions and file digests are recorded in [source-lock.json](./source-lock.json); the additional phrase-source digests are in [vocabulary-corrections.json](./vocabulary-corrections.json). Licences/notices from OpenJLPT and the reference repository are downloaded and verified alongside the data. Keep this provenance with any later published derivative study material, and follow the EDRDG data-freshness and attribution requirements.
