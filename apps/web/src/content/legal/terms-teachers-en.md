# Teacher Marketplace Terms

**English Studio Japan** ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") operates a marketplace that connects independent teachers with adult learners. These **Teacher Marketplace Terms** apply when you offer paid lessons through the platform. They supplement our general [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). If there is a conflict, these Teacher Marketplace Terms control for paid marketplace lessons.

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

---

## 1. Independent teacher status

You are an independent business operator, not our employee or agent. You set your availability, lesson offerings, and pricing (subject to platform rules). You are responsible for your tax, legal, and professional obligations.

Students pay your connected payment account through our checkout. We facilitate discovery, scheduling, messaging, and payment flow.

---

## 2. Platform tier and fees

The platform fee on each paid lesson depends on your **platform tier** and how many paid lessons you have completed in the current monthly volume period (Asia/Tokyo calendar month, counting from your first paid lesson on the platform):

| Tier | Fee schedule |
|------|--------------|
| **Tier 1** | Lessons 1–5: **20%** · Lessons 6–10: **15%** · Lesson 11+: **10%** |
| **Tier 2** | Lessons 1–10: **15%** · Lesson 11+: **10%** |
| **Tier 3** | Flat **10%** on all paid lessons |

Your tier is reviewed periodically based on teaching activity. An administrator may apply a temporary tier override.

The platform fee is collected as an application fee at checkout. It is **not returned to you** when a lesson is refunded under the cancellation policy, unless we separately agree or applicable law requires otherwise. See our [Teacher Refund Policy](/legal/refund/teachers) for details.

---

## 3. Payment account requirements

Before students can pay you for marketplace lessons, you must:

- Accept these Teacher Marketplace Terms and the Teacher Refund Policy in your account settings.
- Complete onboarding with our payment processor (Stripe Connect) and maintain an account in good standing with charges and payouts enabled.

We may restrict paid booking access, payouts, or account features if your payment account has restrictions or repeated refund failures.

---

## 4. Cancellations and refunds

You are responsible for the full refund amount owed to a student when the cancellation policy requires a refund. The platform fee from the original sale is returned to you when a lesson is refunded; this is described in the [Teacher Refund Policy](/legal/refund/teachers).

Rescheduling a lesson does not create a refund entitlement.

By clicking **Accept marketplace terms** in your teacher settings, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Teacher Marketplace Terms, the [Teacher Refund Policy](/legal/refund/teachers), and the payment responsibilities shown in your settings.

We may update these terms. Continued use of paid marketplace features after updated terms are posted constitutes acceptance of the changes.
