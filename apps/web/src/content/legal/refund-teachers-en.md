# Teacher Refund Policy

This **Teacher Refund Policy** explains how refunds work for paid marketplace lessons when you teach on English Studio Japan. It supplements the [Teacher Marketplace Terms](/legal/terms/teachers) and general [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

---

## 1. When refunds apply

A refund is processed automatically only when the lesson cancellation policy marks the cancellation as **refund eligible**.

Unless otherwise shown at checkout, cancellation **more than 48 hours** before the scheduled lesson start is eligible for refund. Cancellations inside that window, no-shows, and reschedules are handled according to the cancellation policy and may not be refund eligible.

When **you** cancel a lesson, the student receives a **full refund** of the lesson price.

---

## 2. Platform fee on refunds

When a lesson is refunded, English Studio Japan **returns the platform fee from the original sale to your connected account in full**. We retain nothing on a lesson that did not take place.

**Example:** A ¥5,000 lesson at Tier 1 (first five lessons, 20% fee) has a ¥1,000 platform fee. If the student is refunded, ¥5,000 is returned to the student and the ¥1,000 platform fee is returned to you.

Stripe does not return its own processing fee on a refunded payment. That fee is the only cost you bear on a refunded lesson.

---

## 3. Student refund amount on cancellations

Every refund is for the **full lesson price**. This is the same whoever cancelled and however close to the lesson the cancellation lands — there is no deduction, and no setting for you to configure.

You issue the refund from your connected payment account.

---

## 4. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the refund amount owed to the student under the cancellation policy.

Refunds are issued from your connected payment account. If your processor balance, bank account, payout status, or account restrictions prevent a refund from completing:

- The refund may fail or require manual recovery.
- You remain responsible for resolving the student's refund.
- We may restrict your paid booking access, payouts, or account features.

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, English Studio Japan is not responsible for your failure to fund or complete a refund, though we may take steps to reduce further risk.

---

## 5. Rescheduling

Rescheduling a lesson to a new time **does not** create a refund entitlement. If a rescheduled lesson is later cancelled under a refund-eligible policy, the refund rules above apply to that cancellation.

---

## 6. Policy updates

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on the platform. Continued use of paid marketplace features after an update constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
