# Student Refund Policy

This **Student Refund Policy** explains when and how refunds work for paid marketplace lessons on English Studio Japan. It supplements the [Student Marketplace Terms](/legal/terms/students) and general [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

---

## 1. When you can get a refund

Refunds are **automatic only when the cancellation policy allows them**.

Unless otherwise shown at checkout, you may receive a refund when you cancel **more than 48 hours** before the scheduled lesson start time and the cancellation policy marks the booking as refund eligible.

Refunds are **not** available for:

- Cancellations inside the policy window (when shown at booking).
- No-shows or late cancellations, as defined by the cancellation policy.
- **Rescheduled lessons** — rescheduling does not create a refund entitlement.

When your **teacher** cancels, you receive a **full refund** of the lesson price.

---

## 2. How much you receive

### Full refund (default)

In most cases, when a refund is eligible you receive **100% of the lesson price** you paid.

### Refund processing fee pass-through

Some teachers enable **Pass the 10% refund processing fee to the student** in their settings. When that setting is on and **you** cancel a refund-eligible booking, you receive **90% of the lesson price** (a flat 10% deduction).

**Example:** You paid ¥5,000 for a lesson. If you cancel under a refund-eligible policy and pass-through is enabled, you receive ¥4,500.

---

## 3. How refunds are processed

Eligible refunds are processed automatically to your original payment method when possible. Processing times depend on your bank or card issuer.

Your transaction is between you and the teacher. English Studio Japan facilitates checkout and refund processing but does not guarantee the teacher's payment account status.

---

## 4. When refunds may fail or delay

A refund may fail or be delayed if the teacher's connected payment account has insufficient funds, payout restrictions, or other processor issues.

In that situation:

- The teacher remains responsible for resolving your refund.
- We may restrict the teacher's paid booking access.
- We are not responsible for the teacher's failure to fund a refund except where required by applicable law.

If a refund cannot be completed automatically, we may mark it for manual recovery and follow up as needed.

---

## 5. Rescheduling

If you reschedule a lesson instead of cancelling, **no refund is issued** for the reschedule itself. If you later cancel the rescheduled lesson under a refund-eligible policy, this policy applies to that cancellation.

---

## 6. Agreement

By completing payment at checkout, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Student Refund Policy and the [Student Marketplace Terms](/legal/terms/students).

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted on the platform.
