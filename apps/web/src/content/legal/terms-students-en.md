# Student Marketplace Terms

**English Studio Japan** ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") operates a marketplace that connects adult learners with independent teachers. These **Student Marketplace Terms** apply when you book and pay for lessons through the platform. They supplement our general [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). If there is a conflict, these Student Marketplace Terms control for paid marketplace lessons.

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026

---

## 1. Relationship with your teacher

Your lesson transaction is **between you and the teacher**. English Studio Japan facilitates teacher discovery, scheduling, checkout, messaging, and related platform operations. We are not the teacher's employer, agent, or guarantor of lesson quality or refund funding except where required by applicable law.

---

## 2. Booking and payment

When you book a paid lesson:

- You agree to the lesson details, price, and cancellation policy shown at checkout.
- You authorize payment through our checkout to the teacher's connected payment account.
- You must accept these Student Marketplace Terms and the [Student Refund Policy](/legal/refund/students) before completing payment.

---

## 3. Cancellations and rescheduling

Refund eligibility is governed by the cancellation policy shown at booking and in the [Student Refund Policy](/legal/refund/students).

Unless otherwise shown at checkout, cancellation **more than 48 hours** before the scheduled lesson start is eligible for a refund when the cancellation policy allows it.

**Rescheduling does not create a refund entitlement.**

When a refund applies, you receive the **full lesson price** you paid.

---

## 4. Refund limitations

Eligible refunds are processed automatically when possible. Refunds may fail or be delayed if the teacher's payment account lacks sufficient funds or has processor restrictions. In that situation, the teacher remains responsible for resolving the refund obligation. We may restrict a teacher's paid booking access to reduce further risk.

See the [Student Refund Policy](/legal/refund/students) for full details.

By checking the agreement box and completing payment at checkout, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Student Marketplace Terms and the [Student Refund Policy](/legal/refund/students).
